Ready to turn to Jesus?

There are no magic words — salvation comes from trusting Jesus, not from reciting a prayer. But if you want somewhere to start, you can tell God something like this:

Lord Jesus, I know I have sinned, and I can't fix that on my own. I believe you died for my sins and rose from the dead. I turn from my sin and trust you as my Lord and Savior. Thank you for forgiving me and giving me new life. Amen.

We'd love to celebrate with you or help with your questions — it takes less than a minute.
